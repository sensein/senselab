## How the twelve were chosen

Four routed-branch profiles (`AIRWAY-only`, `SPEECH+REDACT`, `SPEECH-noredact`, `VOICE`) crossed
with three duration strata (`<5 s`, `5-20 s`, `>60 s`), one recording per cell, each the recording
whose total wall clock is nearest its cell's median so that no pick is an outlier of its own cell.
Deterministic; no sampling. The cells hold between 10 and 12,835 recordings.

## Every node's inputs were there

An earlier probe re-entered the same twelve at TAXONOMY directly, before the driver existed, to
answer whether the store plus its sidecars hold everything the replayed nodes read. Every node
either completed or was skipped by routing; no node errored on any recording; both products
rendered on all twelve. 162.8 s for the twelve replayed, against 2,285.4 s for the same twelve
through the full graph — 14.1 times cheaper, against the 10.4 the corpus-wide node table predicts.
