# Sweep D — Assumptions about who is speaking and what they are doing

Audited all 81 files (both layers) of `src/senselab/audio/workflows/audio_analysis` for constants
and model choices that presume an adult producing fluent connected prose, read against
`specs/20260815-215106-analyze-audio-audit/candidates/sweep-b-computation.md` so an
already-reported "no derivation" defect is not re-filed here under a new label — only its
age-population angle, where one exists and is new, is reported.

Four parallel batches, each reading its files in full:
- Batch 1 (task-calling files: harvesters/asr/embeddings/foreground/background_mask/sound_sources/
  quality/pii/degradation/perturbations, adaptive/backends, adaptive/audio_io — 12 files)
- Batch 2 (speaker-identity/diarization-fusion/speech-presence/acoustic — 21 files)
- Batch 3 (grid/fuse/bookkeeping/reporting, including `compute.py` — 28 files)
- Batch 4 (adaptive/ subpackage — 16 files)

Several batches independently surfaced the same underlying constant from different call sites
(e.g. the 0.30/0.50/0.55/0.70 ECAPA/VoxCeleb cosine-threshold family, and the missing cry/babble
entry in the task→target-event vocabulary). Those are consolidated below into one finding each
rather than filed once per file, per the sweep's own warning against 81 shrugs.

### D-1
- kind: adult-speech-assumption
- location: `src/senselab/audio/workflows/audio_analysis/compute.py:105,533` (`cluster_cosine_threshold: float = 0.5`), reused at `speaker.py:132-135` (`same_speaker_floor=0.30`, `diff_speaker_floor=0.70`, `cluster_cosine_threshold=0.5`), `embeddings.py:262-325` (`merge_threshold=0.55`) and `embeddings.py:611-657` (`calibrate_cosine_uncertainty`'s fallback band, same 0.30/0.70), and `plot.py`'s `_cluster_speakers_by_embedding` (default `cosine_threshold=0.5`)
- assumption: One cosine-similarity family — a same/different-speaker floor of 0.30/0.70 and a merge/cluster threshold of 0.5-0.55 — is applied uniformly, across every recording, to decide same-vs-different speaker for whatever voice(s) are present. The docstrings that justify these numbers cite ECAPA's EER on VoxCeleb, an adult (largely broadcast/celebrity-interview) speaker-verification corpus, and one function's own comment admits the failure mode directly: "different speakers with similar timbre (same gender + age, children, family resemblance) can sit at cos_sim ~0.30."
- population: Holds for adult speakers whose between-speaker separation resembles VoxCeleb's. Does not hold, by the code's own comment, for children or close relatives, whose between-speaker cosine similarity in the same embedding space sits closer together than the adult-derived boundary assumes; unmeasured for infants, whose within-speaker variability across vocal states (calm vs. crying vs. play voice) may itself exceed the between-adult-speaker gap this threshold was set to separate.
- used-as-if-validated: This single threshold family gates `_cluster_pass_speakers`'s synthetic diarization source (merged into `diarization.by_model`), `harvest_speaker_votes`'s same/change-claim validation, and H2 harmonization — all feeding `speaker_count_posterior` and the fused speaker ids in `final/speakers.json` and `final/per_speaker_presence.parquet`, with no population caveat carried into either artifact.
- experiment: Compute the same ECAPA/ResNet embeddings on a corpus of child- or sibling-pair speaker-verification recordings with ground-truth labels, derive the empirical same/different-speaker EER-optimal threshold and the 0.30/0.70 floor's error rate there, and compare against the adult VoxCeleb-derived rate the current constants were set from.

### D-2
- kind: read-speech-assumption
- location: `src/senselab/audio/workflows/audio_analysis/speaker.py:518-543` (`harvest_speaker_votes`, the `fused_words` gate: `if fused_words and coverage[key] <= 0.0: bucket_dict["votes"] = {}`)
- assumption: "No ASR word landed in this bucket" is treated as "no speech to attribute," and on that basis the *entire* votes dict for the bucket is discarded — every diarizer label, embedding cosine, cross-diarizer-agreement entry, and change-point vote already built for it, not just the two attribution voters added afterward. The justifying measurement (22/29 wordless buckets = genuine inter-turn silence) was made on a two-adult conversation.
- population: Holds for connected, lexical speech where "no ASR word" really does mean silence. Does not hold for non-lexical vocalization — infant/toddler babbling, crying, laughing — which no ASR model transcribes as words even when a speaker is acoustically present and diarizers/embeddings placed them there.
- used-as-if-validated: In a caregiver-infant or clinician-toddler recording where the adult speaks in words and the child vocalizes non-lexically, every bucket where only the child is active is silently zeroed out of `harvest_speaker_votes`'s output; downstream `per_speaker_tracks`/`build_speech_presence_tracks` and `final/per_speaker_presence.parquet` emit no row for it at all — the child's speaking time disappears from the record as if it never happened, in exactly the adult+non-verbal-child scenario the workflow's own off-target-speaker-detection goal targets.
- experiment: On a caregiver+infant/toddler recording with ground-truth per-speaker activity annotation (child producing only babble/cry/laugh), measure the fraction of the child's true speaking time dropped by this gate in `final/per_speaker_presence.parquet`, versus the same measurement on an adult-adult control recording.

### D-3
- kind: adult-speech-assumption
- location: `src/senselab/audio/workflows/audio_analysis/speech_presence_link.py:249-287` (`_link_asr`, line 262: `hallucinated = bool(said_something and nsp is not None and nsp >= policy.no_speech_threshold)`)
- assumption: Whisper's `no_speech_prob` head is trusted to flag a spurious transcription as "hallucinated" only when `nsp` reads high — presuming the head is well-calibrated on the input, which holds for the lexical adult/older-child speech Whisper is trained on but is a known failure point for non-lexical vocal sound, where Whisper can emit confident, low-`nsp` fabricated words the gate has no branch to catch.
- population: Holds where the speech-vs-silence distinction Whisper's head was tuned on applies. Does not hold for infant/toddler non-lexical vocalization, largely absent from Whisper's training distribution.
- used-as-if-validated: A confidently-hallucinated transcript over a crying/babbling bucket reports `speaks=True` with high `native_confidence` — a positive vote for "speech here" — and gives the bucket nonzero `word_overlap_s`, which is exactly the condition that prevents D-2's wordless gate from firing, so a non-lexical vocalization can end up attributed to fabricated lexical content rather than recognized as unattributable non-speech vocal activity.
- experiment: Feed Whisper (as configured here) infant-cry, toddler-babble, and laughter clips alongside matched adult non-speech vocal sounds (cough, laugh, sigh); measure the rate of confident (low-`nsp`) fabricated transcriptions per group.

### D-4
- kind: lifespan-gap
- location: `src/senselab/audio/workflows/audio_analysis/speaker_identity.py:469-492` (`evidence_from_passes`/`_distinct_speakers`), consumed via `speaker_count_posterior` and `SpeakerCountPosterior.to_json` (:101-114)
- assumption: The run's "how many speakers" belief (`probabilities`, `modal_count`, `is_multimodal` in `final/speakers.json`) is built entirely from each configured diarizer's reported speaker-label count, weighted by cross-pass stability and physical support — neither of which encodes what population that diarizer's own model was trained or validated on.
- population: The diarizers wired into this workflow are documented and benchmarked overwhelmingly on adult conversational/meeting corpora; their validated behavior on recordings containing infant or toddler voices is unknown to this code and unstated anywhere the count reaches a consumer.
- used-as-if-validated: `modal_count`/`probabilities` is reported as the run's headline speaker-count verdict with no population caveat; a diarizer failure mode specific to child voices (merging two children into one label, or splitting one child's variable voice into two) surfaces only as ordinary posterior uncertainty, indistinguishable from any other source of disagreement.
- experiment: Run the pipeline's diarizers on ground-truth-labeled recordings spanning adult-adult, adult-child, and child-child dyads; measure `modal_count` accuracy and posterior calibration stratified by age composition, and check whether degradation for child-involving dyads shows up as elevated reported uncertainty or is silently absorbed.

### D-5
- kind: lifespan-gap
- location: `src/senselab/audio/workflows/audio_analysis/background_mask.py:42-54,191-211` (`TARGET_EVENT_LABELS`/`target_event_types_for`) and `calibration.py:232-237` (`DEFAULT_DETECTION_MARGIN["mask"]["target_event_types_by_task"]`), against `data/audioset_source_map.json`'s own entries for `"Baby cry, infant cry"` and `"Babbling"`
- assumption: The vocabulary of "what counts as the target's own activity" for a non-speech task is closed over exactly three task types — `speech`, `breath`, `cough` — with no `cry` or `babble` task type anywhere, even though the AudioSet category map this same package loads already has labels for exactly those sounds.
- population: Validated (has an explicit, tested path) for adult/verbal-child fluent-speech and breath/cough elicitation tasks; has no path at all for an infant-cry or toddler-babble elicitation task — a standard developmental recording protocol.
- used-as-if-validated: For a cry- or babble-elicitation recording, `target_event_types_for` returns the speech-only fallback, so a bucket where the child's own cry/babble is the intended target scores `target_confidence≈0` and reads `target_free`; `sound_sources.py`'s own AudioSet map would meanwhile score the same audio's mass on `"Baby cry, infant cry"→people`, so `nontarget_confidence_by_bucket` marks the exact same span `nontarget_active` — the recording's actual target vocalization is reported as background content of interest, the precise misattribution this same mechanism (FR-033a) was built to prevent for breath/cough.
- experiment: Run an annotated infant-cry or toddler free-vocalization recording through the mask/background-scene stage under each declared task label, and measure the fraction of ground-truth cry/babble time marked `target_free`/`nontarget_active` versus the same measurement on an adult breath-task control recording, where `TARGET_EVENT_LABELS` does have an entry.

### D-6
- kind: read-speech-assumption
- location: `src/senselab/audio/workflows/audio_analysis/degradation.py:33-44` (`DEFAULT_ANCHORS`, `snr_clean_db=25.0`/`c50_clean_db=30.0`, documented as "clean conversational or studio speech") and `:129-172` (`scene_degradation`, which takes no `task_type` parameter)
- assumption: The "clean" reference point for quality scoring is fluent conversational/studio speech at ~25 dB+ SNR, applied identically regardless of the recording's target activity — `scene_degradation` has no task-type input at all, unlike `background_mask.py`'s task-aware machinery a few files away in the same package.
- population: Correct for a conversational/read-speech task, where near-silence is itself evidence of poor capture; wrong for a breath, cough, or cry/babble elicitation task (D-5), where the intended target signal is quiet by design and a low SNR relative to a 25 dB "clean speech" anchor is not degradation.
- used-as-if-validated: `quality_snr`/`quality_reverb` feed the run's headline quality/degradation verdict as a single population-general "is this recording good" number, with no task-conditioning anywhere in this file.
- experiment: Record matched breath-task and conversational-task audio from the same device/room, run `harvest_quality_measurements`+`scene_degradation` on both, and check whether the breath-task recording — correctly capturing quiet sounds at its intended level — scores as substantially degraded purely from the SNR gap to the 25 dB conversational anchor.

### D-7
- kind: adult-speech-assumption
- location: `src/senselab/audio/workflows/audio_analysis/compute.py:890-1009` (`_speech_window_mask`)
- assumption: YAMNet's top-1 AudioSet label is treated as authoritative ("veto, not a fallback ladder") for whether an embedding-extraction window counts as speech at all before it is admitted to speaker clustering. The function's own docstring names the failure mode: "YAMNet has known confusions (e.g. tagging child voices as 'Music' or 'Singing'). When that happens, a real-speech window gets dropped from clustering." The stated mitigation is a manual per-run operator action, not a default that protects an unattended run.
- population: Fitted and validated on adult conversational speech. Documented by the codebase itself to misclassify child voices as Music/Singing; the same acoustic mechanism (higher, more variable f0, less canonical prosodic contour) plausibly worsens further down the lifespan for toddler/infant vocalization, which the docstring does not claim to have measured.
- used-as-if-validated: The mask gates which windows reach `_cluster_pass_speakers`, whose output is merged into `diarization.by_model` as a full synthetic diarizer, feeding `speaker_votes`, the speaker-count posterior, `final/per_speaker_presence.parquet` and `final/speakers.json` — a dropped child/infant vocalization reads as "no evidence there" rather than "evidence rejected by an instrument with a known population-specific miss rate."
- experiment: Run YAMNet's top-1 label over a corpus stratified by speaker age band (adult conversational, child connected speech, toddler one-word utterances, infant cry/babble) and measure the false "non-speech" veto rate per band against the adult band's rate.

### D-8
- kind: adult-speech-assumption
- location: `src/senselab/audio/workflows/audio_analysis/compute.py:101,592-593` (`embedding_window_s: float = 2.0`, "ECAPA's recommended minimum")
- assumption: Speaker-embedding extraction for clustering/identity uses a fixed 2.0 s window (1.0 s hop), sized to ECAPA's recommended minimum for a reliable embedding — a figure derived from models benchmarked on sustained adult conversational/read speech, where a single speaker reliably occupies 2+ continuous seconds.
- population: Holds for continuous single-speaker spans of at least ~2 s. A toddler's one-word utterance (commonly under 1 s), a single infant cry bout, or a babbling burst does not fill the window, so the embedding is computed over a mix of the brief target vocalization, silence, and whatever precedes/follows it.
- used-as-if-validated: Windowed embeddings feed the same synthetic-diarization chain as D-7 (`diarization.by_model` → `speaker_votes` → `final/per_speaker_presence.parquet`/`final/speakers.json`), with no signal that a given window's embedding rests on a partially-occupied 2 s span rather than a fully-voiced one — a short vocalization's degraded embedding is scored identically to a fully-supported one.
- experiment: On a corpus with ground-truth vocalization durations spanning infant cries (~0.3-2 s), toddler one-word utterances (~0.3-1 s), and adult conversational turns (>2 s), measure same/different-speaker cosine separation as a function of how much of the 2.0 s window the target speaker's voice actually occupies, and identify the duration below which separation degrades past a usable margin.

### D-9
- kind: lifespan-gap
- location: `src/senselab/audio/workflows/audio_analysis/global_summary.py:29-37,289-306` (`n_speakers` semantics / `single_speaker_uncertainty`)
- assumption: The workflow's headline "single speaker" compliance claim scores any recording with 2+ detected speakers as maximally violating (`single_speaker_uncertainty = 1.0`), presuming the target paradigm is one self-directed solo speaker producing the entire clip (e.g. a clinical voice-biomarker elicitation: sustained phonation, a read passage, a monologue).
- population: Holds for solo, self-administered adult/adolescent recording tasks. Does not hold for caregiver-mediated pediatric elicitation — the standard paradigm for recording infants and toddlers, where a parent, sibling, or clinician necessarily co-occurs (prompting, comforting, eliciting babble or single words), and multiple speakers is the *correct* outcome, not a quality defect.
- used-as-if-validated: `single_speaker_uncertainty` feeds `combined_uncertainty`, the run's bottom-line four-claim verdict; a technically clean caregiver-child recording is scored as maximally noncompliant purely because it correctly contains two speakers, indistinguishable from a recording that was supposed to be solo and was not.
- experiment: Run `compute_run_global_summary` over caregiver-mediated pediatric elicitation recordings alongside solo adult self-report recordings, and compare `single_speaker_uncertainty`/`combined_uncertainty` distributions; if caregiver-mediated recordings cluster near 1.0 regardless of audio quality, the claim needs a per-task expectation (analogous to `expects_speech`) keyed off `task.type` rather than a hardcoded solo-speaker target.

### D-10
- kind: adult-speech-assumption
- location: `src/senselab/audio/workflows/audio_analysis/adaptive/identity_repair.py:81-99` (`detect_change_points`), `:152-244` (`repair_identity`; `min_segment_s=0.25`, `recluster_cosine_threshold=0.45` at :169,:199), consumed via `adaptive/interventions.py:783-820,832-886` (I1/I2), landing in `adaptive/fusion.py:250-330`
- assumption: The change-point/recluster repair presumes speaker turns are recoverable as ≥250 ms segments whose adjacent-window embedding-cosine trajectory has a clean peak at genuine boundaries — i.e. that the underlying embedding model produces stable per-speaker vectors that destabilize only at turn changes, the behavior such models are trained/validated to exhibit on adult conversational corpora.
- population: Holds for adult conversational turn-taking with segments well over the 250 ms floor. Not established for infant/toddler vocalization: individual cries or coos frequently run under 250 ms and are merged into a neighboring segment by construction, and there is no basis for assuming the embedding model's same/different-speaker separability transfers to immature vocal tracts.
- used-as-if-validated: I2's output becomes `refined_identity`, written verbatim into `final/diarization.json`'s segments/clusters (`"refined": true`) and `final/speakers.json`'s hypotheses — the run's authoritative "who is speaking when" — with no field recording that the repair mechanism's validity was established only on adult speech.
- experiment: On a corpus with time-aligned ground truth spanning at least one infant/toddler and one adult per recording, run I1/I2 and compute segment-purity/ARI against ground truth separately for infant-attributed vs. adult-attributed spans, and compare against the same metric on an adult-only conversational corpus.

### D-11
- kind: adult-speech-assumption
- location: `src/senselab/audio/workflows/audio_analysis/adaptive/interventions.py:1059-1131` (I4 `_i4_trigger`/`_i4_execute`), `adaptive/belief.py:1099-1146` (`ALEATORIC_FLOOR_TERMS`/`_attach_floor`), `adaptive/convergence.py:79-86` (irreducibility branch)
- assumption: I4 derives `overlap_posterior` purely from disagreement between diarizers, presuming that when real overlap occurs, diarization models trained/validated mainly on adult multi-speaker corpora will register it as inter-model disagreement rather than folding it into one detected speaker.
- population: Plausible for adult conversational overlap (interruption/backchannel). Not established for infant-caregiver co-vocalization — rapid, non-turn-taking simultaneous crying/babbling with adult speech — where a diarizer validated on adults may simply fail to segment the second voice at all, yielding a *low* `overlap_posterior` that means "the model didn't see two speakers," not "there was no overlap."
- used-as-if-validated: `overlap_posterior` feeds `aleatoric_floor`, and `apply_convergence_marks` uses it to choose between `irreducible_reason: "snr_floor"` and `"no_reduction_under_available_interventions"` — a verdict written into `final/convergence.json` with no caveat that the floor's overlap term is only as good as an adult-validated diarizer's overlap recall in this population.
- experiment: Build or use infant-caregiver recordings with hand-labeled true co-vocalization spans plus a matched adult-adult corpus with labeled overlap; run I4 on both and compare `overlap_posterior` against ground truth per population.

### D-12
- kind: read-speech-assumption
- location: `src/senselab/audio/workflows/audio_analysis/adaptive/interventions.py:430-473` (C9 `_missed_speech_candidates`/`_c9_execute`)
- assumption: C9 only proposes a corrective "speaks" vote in a low-p_voice bucket when `len(families) >= 2` — at least two ASR model families independently produce word-level text there — presuming the vocalization is lexical content that general-purpose ASR systems converge on.
- population: Valid for connected fluent (read or spontaneous) speech, where independent ASR systems tend to agree on at least some words even under noise. Not valid for crying, babbling, or other pre-lexical vocalization, where ASR emits nothing, hallucinates, or disagrees entirely, so the `>=2 families agree` gate essentially never fires regardless of how much genuine vocalization is present.
- used-as-if-validated: A bucket that never gets this corrective vote can still reach `status: "converged"` at low presence — the recorded belief carries no distinction between "converged because it's silence" and "converged because the one correction mechanism for under-confident presence is structurally inapplicable to non-lexical content."
- experiment: On recordings with labeled infant/toddler vocalization at low measured p_voice, measure how often ≥2 ASR families agree on any word inside those spans versus inside labeled adult speech at comparably low p_voice, and report C9's effective recall of true "missed speech" separately for lexical vs. non-lexical ground truth.

### D-13
- kind: lifespan-gap
- location: `src/senselab/audio/workflows/audio_analysis/adaptive/evaluate.py:85-87` (`transcribed`/`untranscribed` split), `:117-130` (`_score_words`, WER restricted to transcribed spans)
- assumption: Untranscribed ground-truth spans are excluded from WER on both sides because "the annotator's own uncertainty is not a reference" — reasonable per-segment, but it leaves the certifying `transcript.wer` metric undefined, unflagged, and unreported for whatever an annotator declined to transcribe, which for non-adult read/spontaneous prose is disproportionately crying, babbling, and other non-lexical vocalization rather than merely hard-to-hear speech.
- population: WER-based validation covers only the fraction of a recording an annotator judged transcribable prose; it reports nothing about, and does not even surface the proportion of, non-lexical content.
- used-as-if-validated: `eval.json`'s `transcript.wer`/`wer_normalized` is the pipeline's headline accuracy figure with no record of what fraction of the recording fell in `untranscribed` spans — a corpus with substantial untranscribable infant/toddler material reports the identical headline WER as an all-adult corpus, silently certifying "the pipeline works" for a population it never scored.
- experiment: Alongside WER, compute and report the fraction of total recording/GT duration in `untranscribed` spans, stratified by available speaker/age metadata, and compare the current WER-only headline against a coverage-adjusted accuracy metric.
