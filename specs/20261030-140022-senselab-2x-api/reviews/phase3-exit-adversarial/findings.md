## Refuted or not filed

- **graph@1.1 with unknown fields:** such a document passes `validate` and is refused only at the digest. Refuted. This is ADR-006's
  later-minor rule (`ADR-006:23-26`, `:414-418`).
- **Execution activities lost on a worker kill:** a killed worker loses the execution activities of the claim it was running. Refuted as a defect. This is
  ADR-010's accepted residual (amendment 2026-11-01: "loses at most the claim it is running or has just committed").
- **`resume` does not compare `profile_digest`:** refuted. ADR-004 allows resume on another site with a fresh placement.
- **Single-client attribute-delay probe and the 3600 s clock tolerance:** these are ADR-003's stated residual (`:260-268`) and are not re-filed.
  R-284 keeps only the roots that are never judged.
- **A batch where one item ran out of memory is never re-sent alone:** already R-258.
