(subagent-driven development: "rule, ledger, keep going"). They are **not design authority**: the owner's
requests ([`owner-requests.md`](owner-requests.md)), the principles and the ADRs are. A ruling that changes a
design decision needs an ADR amendment. Status column: active, superseded, reverted or withdrawn. All rulings were audited in the 2026-11-01 full review
([`reviews/full-review-2026-11-01/`](reviews/full-review-2026-11-01/)), and the owner's decisions D-1..D-7 of that date
([`decisions-2026-11-01.md`](decisions-2026-11-01.md)) set the statuses that name them.
