recording vectors

Two views over recording_vectors.parquet, schema_version 1: a parallel-coordinate view of the corpus where each line is one recording, and a recording view drawn from that recording's compact vectors.

This page renders transcript text and detected PII in place. It reads a file you hand it from your own disk; nothing is uploaded, fetched or stored. Open it from file://, for your own viewing, and do not host it.

recording vectors

facets

Within one facet the chosen values are a union; across facets they are an intersection; and the whole selection intersects the axis brushes. Each value carries available / total — the count under every other facet, and the count over the whole corpus. absent is a value with its own count, so a null is chosen rather than silently kept or silently dropped.

Null is absent, not zero. A line is broken wherever its value on an axis is null; the dashed stubs run to the absent rail below the break, which carries a count and never a position. A categorical axis is ordered categories, not a number line. An m_<name> axis is a mean of m_<name>_n readings, and its caption says how many rows fold more than one. Vectors and matrices are listed in the axis picker but disabled, with the reason on the option.
