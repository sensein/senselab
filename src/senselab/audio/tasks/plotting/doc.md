Audio visualization and playback utilities.

This module provides functions for visualizing audio data: `plot_waveform` (amplitude over time), `plot_specgram` (frequency content over time, linear or mel scale), `plot_waveform_and_specgram` (stacked layout), `plot_aligned_panels` (reusable multi-panel time-aligned visualization supporting waveform, spectrogram, feature overlays, segment annotations, labelled token lanes that carry each token's text on its own bar, on a shared time axis, and text blocks below it), and `plot_range_with_ppg` (one requested time range of a recording as waveform, spectrogram and a PPG phoneme lane on one axis, with every phoneme boundary ruled across all three panels, each argmax phoneme drawn as a full-height washed band named in the strip at the head of the lane, and the competing phonemes' posterior curves overlaid on the lane against a right-hand 0-1 scale). Use `play_audio` for inline audio playback in Jupyter notebooks. All plotting functions support context-aware scaling for different display sizes.
