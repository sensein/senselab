senselab.audio.tasks.speaker_diarization

Speaker diarization

Task Overview

Speaker diarization is the process of segmenting audio recordings by speaker labels, aiming to answer the question: "Who spoke when?"

Models

All supported models (pyannote.audio models from Hugging Face Hub and the NVIDIA nemo softformer) provide state-of-the-art diarization and can be used as follows:

from senselab.audio.data_structures import Audio
from senselab.utils.data_structures.model import HFModel
from senselab.audio.tasks.speaker_diarization.api import diarize_audios

audio = Audio(filepath="path/to/audio_48khz_mono_16bits.wav")
model = HFModel(path_or_uri="nvidia/diar_sortformer_4spk-v1")
results = diarize_audios([audio], model=model)
# results is a list of lists of ScriptLine objects, e.g.:
# [[ScriptLine(speaker="speaker_0", start=0.08, end=4.95), ...]]

We may integrate additional approaches for speaker diarization into the package in the future.

Evaluation

Metrics

The Diarization Error Rate (DER) is the standard metric for evaluating and comparing speaker diarization systems. It is defined as:

DER= (false alarm + missed detection + confusion) / total

where

  • false alarm is the duration of non-speech incorrectly classified as speech, missed detection
  • missed detection is the duration of speech incorrectly classified as non-speech, confusion
  • confusion is the duration of speaker confusion, and total
  • total is the sum over all speakers of their reference speech duration.

Note: DER takes overlapping speech into account. This can lead to increased missed detection rates if the speaker diarization system does not include an overlapping speech detection module.

 44class DiarizationCapabilities:
 45    """What one diarization backend populates, and what its labels mean.
 46
 47    Attributes:
 48        populates_text: Whether the backend fills ``ScriptLine.text``. Without this a
 49            consumer cannot tell "this backend does not transcribe" from "this segment
 50            had no words" — both look like ``text=None``.
 51        speaker_label_kind: ``"identity"`` when ``speaker`` names *who* is talking,
 52            ``"role"`` when it names *what kind* of talker (child-adult emits
 53            CHILD/ADULT/OVERLAP). Role labels must not reach embedding clustering: a
 54            per-role centroid blends distinct speakers under one label.
 55        labels_stable_across_files: Whether label ``"1"`` in one file denotes the same
 56            speaker as ``"1"`` in another. False for any backend that numbers per audio.
 57        max_speakers: The largest speaker count the backend has been observed to *emit*
 58            — a structural ceiling, not a performance one. This is deliberately narrower
 59            than an earlier reading of this field: a backend's counting *accuracy*
 60            (does it report the right count?) degrades independently of whether it can
 61            structurally emit a large count at all, and folding both into one integer
 62            hid that distinction. See ``max_speakers_evidence`` for how a given value
 63            was established.
 64
 65            ``None`` means *no structural ceiling was found* within whatever was tested
 66            — it does **not** mean unlimited, and it does **not** by itself distinguish
 67            "nobody has looked" from "we looked and found no cap" (``max_speakers_evidence``
 68            carries that distinction; see its docstring). Counts distinguishable
 69            *speakers*, not distinct ``speaker`` label values: the child-adult classifier
 70            emits three label values (``CHILD``, ``ADULT``, ``OVERLAP``) but declares
 71            ``max_speakers=2``, because ``OVERLAP`` marks two of the two known talkers
 72            speaking at once, not a third talker.
 73
 74            The seed-17 speaker-ceiling probe (a 160-session, TTS-composed corpus swept
 75            over true speaker counts 1..8; see
 76            ``specs/20260809-112417-speaker-ceiling-probe/``) measured all six backends:
 77            Sortformer and child-adult both plateau at a fixed count (4 and 2) regardless
 78            of how many speakers are actually present — a real structural cap, confirming
 79            what their names/architecture already implied. Pyannote, VibeVoice, MOSS and
 80            DiariZen never plateaued across k=1..8 on that corpus; their predictions kept
 81            tracking (or overshooting) the true count instead of collapsing to one value,
 82            so they declare ``None`` here — "no structural ceiling observed", a measured
 83            claim, not a guess. Every number traces to one corpus and one seed, TTS-composed
 84            with no room acoustics or channel variation — the probe's own profile carries
 85            that caveat and this field points at it rather than restating it loosely; do not
 86            read a probe result as a guarantee about real recordings.
 87        max_speakers_evidence: How ``max_speakers`` was established, machine-readable
 88            rather than only prose in a comment. Exactly :data:`UNMEASURED` when nobody
 89            has run a probe against this backend — the same fact ``max_speakers=None``
 90            alone used to carry before this field existed. Otherwise a string starting
 91            ``"measured:"``, e.g. ``"measured: saturates at 4 on 20/20 k=8 sessions
 92            (probe seed-17)"`` when a real ceiling was found, or ``"measured: no
 93            saturation, emits up to 8 (probe seed-17)"`` when it was not. Checking
 94            ``evidence == UNMEASURED`` vs. ``evidence.startswith("measured:")`` is enough
 95            to recover which case applies without parsing the rest of the string — that
 96            two-state check is what makes this field machine-readable rather than a
 97            comment a reader has to trust. Rejected at construction (see
 98            ``__post_init__``) if it claims :data:`UNMEASURED` while ``max_speakers``
 99            holds a number: a number with no measurement behind it is exactly the
100            unfitted literal this repo's conventions warn against.
101

103            this field is evidence for — is not carried here. It lives with the probe
104            (``specs/20260809-112417-speaker-ceiling-probe/``) and, summarized, in

What one diarization backend populates, and what its labels mean.

Attributes:
  • populates_text: Whether the backend fills ScriptLine.text. Without this a consumer cannot tell "this backend does not transcribe" from "this segment had no words" — both look like text=None.
  • speaker_label_kind: "identity" when speaker names who is talking, "role" when it names what kind of talker (child-adult emits CHILD/ADULT/OVERLAP). Role labels must not reach embedding clustering: a per-role centroid blends distinct speakers under one label.
  • labels_stable_across_files: Whether label "1" in one file denotes the same speaker as "1" in another. False for any backend that numbers per audio.

  • max_speakers: The largest speaker count the backend has been observed to emit — a structural ceiling, not a performance one. This is deliberately narrower than an earlier reading of this field: a backend's counting accuracy (does it report the right count?) degrades independently of whether it can structurally emit a large count at all, and folding both into one integer hid that distinction. See max_speakers_evidence for how a given value was established.

    None means no structural ceiling was found within whatever was tested — it does not mean unlimited, and it does not by itself distinguish "nobody has looked" from "we looked and found no cap" (max_speakers_evidence carries that distinction; see its docstring). Counts distinguishable speakers, not distinct speaker label values: the child-adult classifier emits three label values (CHILD, ADULT, OVERLAP) but declares max_speakers=2, because OVERLAP marks two of the two known talkers speaking at once, not a third talker.

    The seed-17 speaker-ceiling probe (a 160-session, TTS-composed corpus swept over true speaker counts 1..8; see specs/20260809-112417-speaker-ceiling-probe/) measured all six backends: Sortformer and child-adult both plateau at a fixed count (4 and 2) regardless of how many speakers are actually present — a real structural cap, confirming what their names/architecture already implied. Pyannote, VibeVoice, MOSS and DiariZen never plateaued across k=1..8 on that corpus; their predictions kept tracking (or overshooting) the true count instead of collapsing to one value, so they declare None here — "no structural ceiling observed", a measured claim, not a guess. Every number traces to one corpus and one seed, TTS-composed with no room acoustics or channel variation — the probe's own profile carries that caveat and this field points at it rather than restating it loosely; do not read a probe result as a guarantee about real recordings.

  • max_speakers_evidence: How max_speakers was established, machine-readable rather than only prose in a comment. Exactly UNMEASURED when nobody has run a probe against this backend — the same fact max_speakers=None alone used to carry before this field existed. Otherwise a string starting "measured:", e.g. "measured: saturates at 4 on 20/20 k=8 sessions (probe seed-17)" when a real ceiling was found, or "measured: no saturation, emits up to 8 (probe seed-17)" when it was not. Checking evidence == UNMEASURED vs. evidence.startswith("measured:") is enough to recover which case applies without parsing the rest of the string — that two-state check is what makes this field machine-readable rather than a comment a reader has to trust. Rejected at construction (see __post_init__) if it claims UNMEASURED while max_speakers holds a number: a number with no measurement behind it is exactly the unfitted literal this repo's conventions warn against.

    Counting accuracy — a related but separate fact from the structural bound this field is evidence for — is not carried here. It lives with the probe (specs/20260809-112417-speaker-ceiling-probe/) and, summarized, in model_registry.yaml's recommended_for for each backend.

  • honors_speaker_hints: Whether num_speakers/min_speakers/max_speakers passed to diarize_audios() do anything. Five of six ignore them.
