senselab.audio.tasks.speech_to_text_ensemble

Speech-to-text ensemble (transcript fusion)

Combine word-timestamped transcripts from multiple ASR systems into a single consensus transcript with per-word confidence and alternates (ROVER-style time-slot voting). Promoted from the adaptive uncertainty workflow (spec 20260723-225523-dynamic-uncertainty-workflow, architecture-review T050); model-independent and dependency-free.

  • fuse_word_streams(word_streams, weights=...) — time-slot voting; weights lets callers down-weight correlated systems (e.g. senselab's model-family weights) or weight by validation accuracy.
  • Per-word corroboration in [0, 1] — optional external evidence that something was said there, supplied by the caller. It enters the vote weight and the coverage term (for a one-member slot share is identically 1.0, so vote weight alone would be a no-op on exactly the words the mechanism exists for) and is floored at MIN_CORROBORATION: an uncorroborated word is attenuated, never dropped. Absent or None means unmeasured and applies no discount. Alternates are gated on a second, uncorroborated tally, so attenuation can decide who wins but never who is recorded.
  • load_calibrator(profile) — logistic / piecewise confidence calibration maps.
  • iter_word_leaves(node) — recursive word-leaf extraction from serialized ScriptLine trees (dicts); for ScriptLine instances use ScriptLine.iter_leaves().

212    """Fuse per-system word streams into one consensus word list (ROVER-lite).
213
214    Words across systems are grouped into time slots (time-overlap fraction ≥
215    ``slot_overlap`` or midpoint distance ≤ ``slot_mid_tol_s``); each slot votes
216    on the normalized text with ``weights[system] × word confidence ×
217    corroboration``. Coverage penalizes slots that only a subset of
218    systems witnessed (abstention is evidence). Optional ``speaker_at`` attributes
219    speakers; ``calibrator`` maps raw confidences through a calibration profile.
220
221    **A word is doubtful in two independent ways (D-27), and reports both.**
222
223    - ``existence_confidence`` — was this said, and is this the text: ``share × coverage``, times
224      the members' own confidence **when any member reports one**. It used to be
225      ``share × member_conf × coverage`` with ``member_conf`` defaulting to 1.0, and every default
226      recognizer reports no confidence at all — so the term was always 1.0 and 61 of 62 words on a
227      real run came out at exactly 1.0. Absent is now absent: ``member_confidence`` is ``None`` and
228      the term drops out rather than silently reading as certainty.
229    - ``temporal_confidence`` — is it *here*: agreement between **timing sources** on the word's
230      onset and offset, anchored to the word's own duration. ``None`` for a single timing source,
231      which has nobody to agree with; a lone source reporting perfect temporal confidence is the
232      same manufactured certainty in the other field.
233
234    ``confidence`` remains, and is the joint — their product where both exist. The asr axis is a
235    resampling of that joint onto the time grid, which is why the two parts have to survive
236    separately as far as the axis: a word every model agrees on but times differently and a word
237    the models disagree about are different findings, and the product alone cannot tell them apart.
238
239    **Timing sources, not models.** Two transcripts timed by one aligner agree about onset by
240    construction (Canary-Qwen is timed by Qwen's aligner), so members are grouped by
241    ``timestamp_source`` before the spread is measured. Provenance is the only thing that can see
242    this: an aligner is not a ``Source``, so source-closure intersection cannot (D-20).
243
244    **Corroboration** is an optional per-word ``corroboration`` field in ``[0, 1]``: external
245    evidence, measured by the caller, that something was said there. It enters in exactly two
246    places — the vote weight and the coverage term — and nowhere else:
247
248    - The **coverage** term is the load-bearing one. ``share`` is identically 1.0 for a one-member
249      slot, so a factor entering only the vote weight is a provable no-op on precisely the case
250      this mechanism exists for: a word one model produced where nothing corroborates it.
251    - ``member_conf`` is deliberately **untouched**. It reports what the models said about
252      themselves; folding corroboration into it would make one number mean two measurements, and
253      would corrupt the field a calibration profile is fitted against.
254
255    Absent or ``None`` corroboration means *unmeasured* and is treated as 1.0, never as 0.0 — a
256    factor nobody gathered must not act as a discount.
257
258    Alternates are tallied twice. The corroborated tally decides the winner, its ``share`` and its
259    confidence; a second tally without corroboration decides *what gets recorded*. Attenuation may
260    decide who wins; it may never decide who is written down, or a doubted reading disappears
261    through the alternates gate as completely as deleting it would have.
262
263    Args:
264        word_streams: ``{system_id: [{"text", "start", "end", "confidence"?, "corroboration"?}]}``.
265        weights: Optional per-system weight, e.g. model-family weights.
266        slot_overlap: Minimum time-overlap fraction to join an existing slot.

271        speaker_at: Optional ``(t) -> speaker_id | None``.
272        calibrator: Optional ``(confidence) -> calibrated``.

274            aligned position, each word carrying its own ``model``. Supply these from a sequence
275            alignment when insertions matter: time-overlap grouping cannot tell an inserted filler
276            from a competing reading of its neighbour, and silently drops the filler while
277            duplicating the neighbour. When given, ``slot_overlap`` and ``slot_mid_tol_s`` are
278            unused.
279        text_similarity: Optional ``(text_a, text_b) -> [0, 1]`` used to grade a member's
280            agreement with the winning text instead of counting exact matches. Omitted means a
281            mismatch contributes nothing, which is the historical behaviour — the caller supplies
282            the measure so this routine stays model- and dependency-independent.

362        # contribution depends on how close its text is to the winning text, which is not decidable
363        # while the votes are still being counted.
364        weighted_members: list[tuple[Mapping[str, Any], str, float]] = []
365        for m in slot["members"]:

367            corr = _corroboration_of(m, float(min_corroboration))
368            member_corroboration[m["model"]] = corr
369            system_weight = weights.get(m["model"], 1.0)
370            base = system_weight * float(m.get("confidence", 1.0))
371            wt = base * (1.0 if corr is None else corr)
372            weighted_members.append((m, key, wt))
373            total_w += wt
374            total_w_uncorroborated += base
375            # Coverage over the *members* rather than the model names, so the corroboration of the
376            # word each system actually produced reaches the abstention term. The denominator stays
377            # the ensemble at full trust.
378            coverage_mass += system_weight * (1.0 if corr is None else corr)
379            t = tally.setdefault(

383                    "weight_uncorroborated": 0.0,

402        win_key, win = ranked[0]
403        # Graded agreement, not a match count. Every member contributes in proportion to how close
404        # its text is to the winning text, so a recognizer that misheard a vowel is distinguishable
405        # from one that produced a different word. Measured before this: "cat" vs "cot" and "cat"
406        # vs "elephant" both scored 0.5, because a member was either the winning string or not, and
407        # accuracy over a 62-word run took two distinct values.
408        #
409        # Exact matches score 1.0 by definition, so unanimous slots stay at exactly 1.0 and no run's
410        # agreeing numbers move. With no ``text_similarity`` supplied a mismatch scores 0.0 — the
411        # previous behaviour — rather than silently falling back to grapheme overlap, which is a
412        # different measurement and would change what the field means with nothing recording it.
413        agreement = 0.0
414        # Retained per member, not only accumulated. ``share`` is the *weighted mean* of these, and a
415        # mean is not a distribution: a slot where two recognizers say the winning word and one says
416        # something else, and a slot where all three half-agree, produce the same ``share`` and are
417        # different findings. The consumer that needs them apart is the asr axis, whose
418        # ``epistemic_uncertainty`` was structurally 0.0 because the only thing reaching it was this
419        # mean — so the recognizers' disagreement, which is exactly the cross-source spread that term
420        # exists to measure, had been averaged away one layer earlier.
421        member_agreement: dict[str, float] = {}
422        for member, member_key, member_wt in weighted_members:
423            if member_key == win_key:
424                agreed = 1.0
425            elif text_similarity is not None:
426                agreed = max(0.0, min(1.0, float(text_similarity(str(member["text"]), str(win["display"])))))

428                # No similarity function supplied: a mismatch is a mismatch, the same 0.0 the
429                # accumulator below has always used. Not a silent fallback to grapheme overlap.

432            agreement += member_wt * agreed
433        share = agreement / total_w

436        # out of the product instead of entering it as 1.0 — which is a claim of certainty made on
437        # behalf of models that said nothing about themselves.
438        member_conf = sum(win["confs"]) / len(win["confs"]) if win["confs"] else None
439        coverage = min(1.0, coverage_mass / ensemble_weight)
440        existence_conf = share * coverage * (1.0 if member_conf is None else member_conf)
441        onset_conf, offset_conf, timing_sources = _temporal_agreement(slot["members"])

443        # edges are reported separately because they are separate findings, and a renderer colouring
444        # one box by the pooled value cannot show which end is in doubt.
445        edges = [c for c in (onset_conf, offset_conf) if c is not None]
446        temporal_conf = min(edges) if edges else None
447        raw_conf = existence_conf if temporal_conf is None else existence_conf * temporal_conf
448        win_corr = (win["corr_num"] / win["corr_den"]) if win["corr_den"] > 0 else None
449        word = {
450            "text": win["display"],
451            "start": round(slot["start"], 4),
452            "end": round(slot["end"], 4),
453            "confidence": round(calibrator(raw_conf), 4) if calibrator else round(raw_conf, 4),
454            "existence_confidence": round(existence_conf, 4),
455            "temporal_confidence": None if temporal_conf is None else round(temporal_conf, 4),
456            "onset_confidence": None if onset_conf is None else round(onset_conf, 4),
457            "offset_confidence": None if offset_conf is None else round(offset_conf, 4),
458            "member_confidence": None if member_conf is None else round(member_conf, 4),
459            "timing_sources": timing_sources,
460            "coverage": round(coverage, 4),
461            "corroboration": None if win_corr is None else round(win_corr, 6),
462            # Each recognizer's agreement with the winning text, in ``[0, 1]``: 1.0 for an exact
463            # match, the phoneme similarity otherwise. ``share`` is their weighted mean, so these are
464            # its decomposition rather than a second body of evidence — which is what lets the asr
465            # axis carry one signal per recognizer without double-counting (D-21 rule 6).
466            "member_agreement": {model: round(value, 6) for model, value in sorted(member_agreement.items())},
467            "member_corroboration": {
468                model: (None if value is None else round(value, 6))
469                for model, value in sorted(member_corroboration.items())
470            },
471            "sources": sorted(set(win["models"])),
472            "alternates": [],
473            "flags": (["single_source"] if len(slot["models"]) == 1 else []),
474        }
475        # Gated on the uncorroborated tally: whether a reading is *recorded* must not depend on how
476        # far it was corroborated, or attenuation restores the erasure through the alternates gate.
477        if share_uncorroborated < winner_margin:
478            for _key, alt in ranked[1:]:
479                alt_share_uncorroborated = alt["weight_uncorroborated"] / total_w_uncorroborated
480                if alt_share_uncorroborated >= alternate_min_share:
481                    alt_corr = (alt["corr_num"] / alt["corr_den"]) if alt["corr_den"] > 0 else None
482                    word["alternates"].append(
483                        {
484                            "text": alt["display"],
485                            "share": round(alt["weight"] / total_w, 4),
486                            "share_uncorroborated": round(alt_share_uncorroborated, 4),
487                            "models": sorted(set(alt["models"])),
488                            "corroboration": None if alt_corr is None else round(alt_corr, 6),
489                        }
490                    )
491        if speaker_at is not None:
492            word["speaker"] = speaker_at(slot["mid"])
493        fused.append(word)

Fuse per-system word streams into one consensus word list (ROVER-lite).

Words across systems are grouped into time slots (time-overlap fraction ≥ slot_overlap or midpoint distance ≤ slot_mid_tol_s); each slot votes on the normalized text with weights[system] × word confidence × corroboration. Coverage penalizes slots that only a subset of systems witnessed (abstention is evidence). Optional speaker_at attributes speakers; calibrator maps raw confidences through a calibration profile.

A word is doubtful in two independent ways (D-27), and reports both.

  • existence_confidence — was this said, and is this the text: share × coverage, times the members' own confidence when any member reports one. It used to be share × member_conf × coverage with member_conf defaulting to 1.0, and every default recognizer reports no confidence at all — so the term was always 1.0 and 61 of 62 words on a real run came out at exactly 1.0. Absent is now absent: member_confidence is None and the term drops out rather than silently reading as certainty.
  • temporal_confidence — is it here: agreement between timing sources on the word's onset and offset, anchored to the word's own duration. None for a single timing source, which has nobody to agree with; a lone source reporting perfect temporal confidence is the same manufactured certainty in the other field.

confidence remains, and is the joint — their product where both exist. The asr axis is a resampling of that joint onto the time grid, which is why the two parts have to survive separately as far as the axis: a word every model agrees on but times differently and a word the models disagree about are different findings, and the product alone cannot tell them apart.

Timing sources, not models. Two transcripts timed by one aligner agree about onset by construction (Canary-Qwen is timed by Qwen's aligner), so members are grouped by timestamp_source before the spread is measured. Provenance is the only thing that can see this: an aligner is not a Source, so source-closure intersection cannot (D-20).

Corroboration is an optional per-word corroboration field in [0, 1]: external evidence, measured by the caller, that something was said there. It enters in exactly two places — the vote weight and the coverage term — and nowhere else:

  • The coverage term is the load-bearing one. share is identically 1.0 for a one-member slot, so a factor entering only the vote weight is a provable no-op on precisely the case this mechanism exists for: a word one model produced where nothing corroborates it.
  • member_conf is deliberately untouched. It reports what the models said about themselves; folding corroboration into it would make one number mean two measurements, and would corrupt the field a calibration profile is fitted against.

Absent or None corroboration means unmeasured and is treated as 1.0, never as 0.0 — a factor nobody gathered must not act as a discount.

Alternates are tallied twice. The corroborated tally decides the winner, its share and its confidence; a second tally without corroboration decides what gets recorded. Attenuation may decide who wins; it may never decide who is written down, or a doubted reading disappears through the alternates gate as completely as deleting it would have.

122
123    Operates on dicts/lists (JSON artifacts or ``ScriptLine.model_dump()``); for
124    ``ScriptLine`` *instances* use :meth:`ScriptLine.iter_leaves`.
125    """
126    out: list[dict[str, Any]] = []
127    if isinstance(node, list):
128        for item in node:

131    if not isinstance(node, dict):
132        return out
133    chunks = node.get("chunks")
134    if isinstance(chunks, list) and chunks:
135        for c in chunks:
136            out.extend(iter_word_leaves(c))
137        return out
138    text, start, end = node.get("text"), node.get("start"), node.get("end")
139    if text and start is not None and end is not None:
140        try:
141            word = {"text": str(text).strip(), "start": float(start), "end": float(end)}
142        except (TypeError, ValueError):
143            return out
144        score = node.get("score")
145        if isinstance(score, (int, float)) and 0.0 <= float(score) <= 1.0:
146            word["confidence"] = float(score)
147        # Timing provenance travels with the word, because that is where the consumer needs it:
148        # ``_temporal_agreement`` groups members by who produced their times, and a word that
149        # arrives without it is counted as its own independent timing source.

151            value = node.get(field)
152            if value:

154        if word["text"]:
155            out.append(word)

161
162    Supported shapes: ``{"type": "logistic", "a": float, "b": float}`` applies
163    ``sigmoid(a · logit(c) + b)``; ``{"type": "piecewise", "x": [...], "y": [...]}``
164    linearly interpolates. ``None``/missing → no calibration.
165    """
166    if not profile:
167        return None
168    kind = str(profile.get("type") or "")
169    if kind == "logistic":
170        a, b = float(profile["a"]), float(profile["b"])
171
172        def _logistic(c: float) -> float:
173            c = min(1.0 - 1e-6, max(1e-6, float(c)))
174            z = a * math.log(c / (1.0 - c)) + b
175            return round(1.0 / (1.0 + math.exp(-z)), 4)
176
177        return _logistic
178    if kind == "piecewise":
179        xs = [float(v) for v in profile["x"]]
180        ys = [float(v) for v in profile["y"]]
181        if len(xs) != len(ys) or len(xs) < 2:
182            raise ValueError("piecewise calibration profile needs matching x/y with >= 2 knots")
183
184        def _piecewise(c: float) -> float:
185            c = float(c)
186            if c <= xs[0]:
187                return round(ys[0], 4)
188            for i in range(1, len(xs)):
189                if c <= xs[i]:
190                    t = (c - xs[i - 1]) / max(1e-9, xs[i] - xs[i - 1])
191                    return round(ys[i - 1] + t * (ys[i] - ys[i - 1]), 4)
192            return round(ys[-1], 4)
193
194        return _piecewise
195    raise ValueError(f"unknown calibration profile type: {kind!r}")
