senselab.audio.tasks.turn_taking_projection

Turn-taking projection

Voice Activity Projection (VAP) for two-person conversations, and the dyadic turn-taking measures read from it.

from senselab.audio.data_structures import Audio
from senselab.audio.tasks.turn_taking_projection import (
    project_voice_activity, separate_dyad, summarize_projection,
)

mix = Audio(filepath="call.wav")                          # mono, both speakers
channel_a, channel_b = separate_dyad(mix, diarization)    # list[ScriptLine] with speaker/start/end
vap = project_voice_activity((channel_a, channel_b))      # VAPResult, 10 frames/s
summary = summarize_projection(vap, turns, ipus)          # dict: per speaker, overall, events

A stereo recording with one speaker per channel goes straight to project_voice_activity(stereo).

Steps

separate_dyad turns a mono mix into one 16 kHz channel per speaker; the caller's diarization defines who is who.

method A channel is Model, licence
masking (default) the mix under that speaker's diarization segments, 50 ms raised-cosine ramps outside each segment; overlap goes to both none
mossformer2 a MossFormer2_SS_16K source matched to the speaker alibabasglab/MossFormer2_SS_16K, Apache-2.0
pyannote a speech-separation-ami-1.0 source matched to the speaker pyannote/speech-separation-ami-1.0, MIT, gated

Separators run on 60 s windows with 2 s cross-fades; each window's sources are matched to the caller's speakers by energy share on frames where only one of them talks, so a separator's arbitrary output order cannot swap the channels for the rest of a recording.

On synthetic dialogues, masking reproduced the clean-stereo projection (p_now correlation 0.99 with the true segments, 0.98 with pyannote diarization); MossFormer2_SS_16K left most of both speakers in one source (0.06) and ran at 0.14x real time on CPU. pyannote is implemented but was not measured. Numbers and method: specs/20261017-110000-dyadic-turn-taking/.

project_voice_activity runs MaAI's stereo VAP (maai-kyoto/vap_en_kyoto, multi-condition training by default) and its backchannel model (maai-kyoto/vap_bc_en) in an isolated maai venv, on CPU. Both checkpoints are MIT and pinned to commits; the CPC encoder they build on is checked by SHA-256. The backchannel model predicts the second channel's backchannels, so it runs once per listener; the three runs go in parallel, one thread each.

VAPResult.frames columns: time (end of the audio the frame has seen), p_now_a/b and p_future_a/b (probability that each speaker holds the floor 0-0.6 s / 0.6-2 s ahead; a + b = 1), vad_a/b, and p_bc_a/b (probability that the speaker backchannels now).

summarize_projection reads the frames at the caller's events:

  • shifts (consecutive turns by different speakers): the next speaker's mean p_now over the 0.5 s before the floor changed, whether it exceeded 0.5, and for how long it had (lead_s);
  • pauses (mutual silences of at least 0.25 s between IPUs): the other speaker's p_now 50-250 ms into the pause, called shift or hold and scored against what happened (accuracy, balanced accuracy, AUC). A pause holding a backchannel is reported but not scored;
  • backchannel opportunities (peaks of a listener's p_bc above 0.5, at least 1 s apart): matched to the backchannels the listener produced (-0.5 to +1 s): responsiveness (opportunities answered) and the fraction of the listener's backchannels timed to one.

Backchannels can be passed in; otherwise a listener IPU of at most 1 s inside the other speaker's turn is taken as one. Every constant is in data/parameters.json with its derivation, and every event row is returned with its continuous score.

Limits

  • Mono input loses what VAP was trained on, each speaker's own channel. Under masking a channel is silent wherever the diarization says that speaker is silent, so diarization errors (missed short backchannels, wrong speaker at overlaps) become VAP input errors, and overlapped speech is in both channels.
  • The checkpoints are English and were trained on dialogue recorded one speaker per channel. Telephone/Zoom codecs and cross-talk were not measured here.
  • The measures are model readings at annotated moments, not ground truth about the speakers.

124    """Run Voice Activity Projection (and backchannel prediction) over a two-speaker dyad.
125
126    Args:
127        stereo_or_pair: A stereo ``Audio`` with one speaker per channel, or two mono ``Audio``
128            (e.g. from :func:`separate_dyad`). Resampled to 16 kHz; trimmed to the shorter channel.
129        model: A key of :data:`maai.VAP_CHECKPOINTS`.
130        backchannel_model: A key of :data:`maai.BC_CHECKPOINTS`, or ``None`` to skip the two
131            backchannel runs.
132        device: CPU or CUDA. Each run is single-threaded; on CPU, ``num_workers`` runs go at once.
133        speakers: Labels for the two channels. ``None`` takes ``metadata["speaker"]`` of a pair from
134            :func:`separate_dyad`, else ``("a", "b")``.
135        num_workers: Concurrent runs (the VAP run and the two backchannel runs).
136
137    Returns:
138        A :class:`VAPResult` at 10 frames per second.
139
140    Raises:
141        ValueError: On a malformed input or an unknown checkpoint, or if ``device`` is MPS.
142    """
143    if device not in (DeviceType.CPU, DeviceType.CUDA):
144        raise ValueError(f"MaAI runs on CPU or CUDA, not {device}")
145    a, b, labels, source = _split_input(stereo_or_pair, speakers)
146    jobs = maai.plan_jobs(model, backchannel_model)
147    began = time.monotonic()
148    arrays, timing = maai.run_maai(a, b, jobs, device=device, num_workers=num_workers)
149    wall = time.monotonic() - began
150    vap = arrays["vap"]
151    n = vap.shape[0]
152    frames = pd.DataFrame(vap, columns=list(maai.VAP_COLUMNS))
153    frames.insert(0, "time", (np.arange(n, dtype=np.float64) + 1.0) / maai.FRAME_RATE)
154    if backchannel_model is not None:
155        frames["p_bc_a"] = arrays["bc_a"][:n, 0]
156        frames["p_bc_b"] = arrays["bc_b"][:n, 0]
157    duration = a.waveform.shape[-1] / SAMPLING_RATE
158    models = {
159        job.name: {
160            "repo_id": job.checkpoint.repo_id,
161            "revision": job.checkpoint.revision,
162            "filename": job.checkpoint.filename,
163            "licence": job.checkpoint.licence,
164            "channel_1": "b" if job.swap else "a",
165        }
166        for job in jobs
167    }
168    models["encoder"] = {"url": maai.CPC_URL, "sha256": maai.CPC_SHA256}
169    flat_timing = {f"{name}_{k}": v for name, t in timing.items() for k, v in t.items()}
170    flat_timing.update({"wall_s": wall, "audio_s": duration, "x_real_time": duration / wall if wall > 0 else 0.0})
171    return VAPResult(

173        speakers=labels,
174        frames=frames,
175        models=models,
