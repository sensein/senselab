senselab.video.tasks.scene_description

Scene description

Describes the environment visible in a recorded video call or video interview: the call layout, and for each participant tile whether the camera is on, the setting, background, lighting, other people and distractions.

from senselab.video.tasks.scene_description import default_backend, describe_video_scenes

backend = default_backend()  # MLX Qwen3-VL-4B on Apple silicon, transformers Qwen3-VL-4B elsewhere
reports = describe_video_scenes(["interview1.mp4", "interview2.mp4"], backend=backend, interval_s=180)
summary = reports[0].summary
summary.categorical["primary_setting"].mode, summary.categorical["layout"].consistency
summary.change_points  # layout / tile-count changes, other people appearing or leaving

The pipeline has four stages, each usable alone:

  1. sample_frames decodes the file once (PyAV) and keeps one frame per interval_s plus frames whose colour histogram jumps by more than scene_threshold, downscaled to long_side.
  2. frame_metrics measures brightness, contrast, blur (variance of the Laplacian), colourfulness, the near-black area, the letterbox/pillarbox bar area, the camera-off area and the Haar-cascade face count. camera_off_fraction excludes centred edge bars; given tile boxes (describe_video_scene(..., tile_boxes=[t.box for t in layout.tiles]) with a layout from facial_analysis.detect_gallery_tiles) it is the fraction of camera-off tiles instead, with the per-tile flags in tiles_camera_off. Thresholds are in data/cv_metrics.yaml.
  3. describe_frames asks the VLM for strict JSON matching SceneDescription, validates it, and sends an invalid completion back once with the error; parse_ok records the outcome.
  4. aggregate reports per-field modes, means and consistency (fraction of frames agreeing with the mode), distraction rates and change points.

The MLX backend runs in the mlx-vlm subprocess venv, whose worker keeps the model resident across frames and videos. Schema, model choice and measurements: specs/20261017-104000-video-scene-description/design.md.
