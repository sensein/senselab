senselab.text.tasks.conversation_analysis

Conversation analysis

Turn-taking, backchannels, overlaps and interruptions, disfluencies, rule-based dialogue acts, engagement/politeness markers and entrainment, computed from a diarized, word-timestamped transcript of a conversation between two or more speakers. Pure Python: no model is loaded.

from senselab.text.tasks.conversation_analysis import analyze_conversation

words = [
    {"speaker": "A", "start": 0.0, "end": 0.3, "text": "So"},
    {"speaker": "A", "start": 0.35, "end": 0.6, "text": "[UM]"},
    ...
]
result = analyze_conversation(words)
tables = result.to_dataframes()   # words, ipus, turns, overlaps, floor_transfers, disfluencies,
                                  # dialogue_acts, markers, summary (+ entrainment)
per_speaker = result.summary()    # {speaker: {metric: value}}

Words may be ScriptLines (or lines whose chunks are words) or mappings with speaker, start, end, text and optional confidence, in any order. Fillers (uh, um, [UH], ...) are kept verbatim everywhere: no step drops or rewrites a word.

Pipeline

  1. IPUs — a speaker's words joined across silences shorter than ipu_gap_s.
  2. Backchannels — short IPUs that are lexically backchannel-like or wholly overlapped, and that leave the floor with the other speaker.
  3. Turns — non-backchannel IPUs grouped per floor; an IPU wholly inside another speaker's turn is an embedded turn that never held the floor.
  4. Floor transfers — FTO between consecutive floor-holding turns; gap / overlap / no-gap-no-overlap.
  5. Overlaps — every pair of overlapping IPUs: backchannel, non-competitive (terminal) or competitive (interruption, successful or unsuccessful).
  6. Dialogue acts — turns split at sentence-final punctuation and labelled by label_dialogue_acts(segments, labeller=None); pass any DialogueActLabeller to replace the rules. A turn's act is primary_act(segments): backchannel/agreement never head a turn that also holds a question, directive or statement with substantive_min_content_words content words.
  7. Disfluencies — filled pauses (with position, surrounding pauses and what follows), repetitions, cut-offs and prolongations.
  8. Markers — discourse markers, hedges, politeness strategies, pronouns and lexical alignment.
  9. Entrainment — compute_entrainment(turn_features) on any per-turn numeric series, turn_entrainment(turns, features) on an analysis' floor-holding turns with a turn_id-keyed feature table, or analyze_conversation(..., turn_features=df).

Thresholds live in data/defaults.yaml (each with its derivation) and lexicons in data/lexicons.yaml; override either through ConversationAnalysisConfig. Definitions and references for every metric are in specs/20261017-120000-conversation-analysis/spec.md.

34    features: Optional[Sequence[str]] = None,

37    time_col: str = "start",

41    """Entrainment of per-turn feature series between speakers (Levitan & Hirschberg 2011 style).
42

44    speakers: the earlier is the partner's turn ``y_{t-1}``, the later the target's turn ``x_t``.
45
46    * proximity: mean over pairs of (mean ``|x_t - y_j|`` over the partner's other turns) minus
47      ``|x_t - y_{t-1}|``; positive when a turn is closer to the turn it answers than to the partner's
48      turns in general.
49    * convergence: Pearson r between ``|x_t - y_{t-1}|`` and the target turn's time; negative when the

51    * synchrony: Pearson r between ``x_t`` and ``y_{t-1}``.

54    the differences against time (convergence). Rows with a missing value are dropped per feature
55    before pairs are formed.

64

67    """

70            c
71            for c in turn_features.select_dtypes("number").columns

78            turn_features[[speaker_col, time_col, feature]]

81            .reset_index(drop=True)
82        )
83        speakers = frame[speaker_col].astype(str).to_numpy()
84        values = frame[feature].astype(float).to_numpy()

 52
 53    The holder is the speaker whose IPU started first (ties: lower IPU id). An overlap is
 54    ``"backchannel"`` when either IPU is a backchannel; ``"non_competitive"`` when it starts within
 55    ``terminal_overlap_window_s`` of the end of the holder's IPU; otherwise ``"competitive"``. A

 57    ``interruption_yield_window_s`` of the overlap onset and the overlapper's IPU ends after it.
 58
 59    Args:
 60        ipus: IPUs with backchannels marked.

 62
 63    Returns:
 64        Overlaps sorted by onset.
 65    """
 66    ordered = sorted(ipus, key=lambda i: (i.start, i.id))
 67    episodes: List[Overlap] = []
 68    for a_index, holder in enumerate(ordered):
 69        for over in ordered[a_index + 1 :]:
 70            if over.start >= holder.end:
 71                break
 72            if over.speaker == holder.speaker:
 73                continue

 86                outcome = "successful" if yielded else "unsuccessful"

 89                    id=0,
 90                    start=start,
 91                    end=end,

 93                    overlapper_speaker=over.speaker,

 96                    overlap_type=kind,

Entrainment of per-turn features over the floor-holding turns of a conversation.

Embedded turns are dropped; each remaining turn's speaker and start come from turns and its numeric features from the features row with the same turn_id. The series then go through compute_entrainment().

Arguments:
  • turns: Turns, e.g. ConversationAnalysis.turns.
  • features: Per-turn numeric features with a turn_id column matching Turn.id; any speaker/start columns are ignored in favour of the turns'. Turns without a row get missing values.
  • n_surrogates: Surrogate shuffles for the baseline.
  • seed: Seed for the surrogates.
Returns:

One result per numeric feature column.

Raises:
  • ValueError: features has no turn_id column.
